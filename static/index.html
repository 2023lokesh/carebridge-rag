What is CareBridge?

A bridge between vaccine questions and source information.

CareBridge is an academic vaccine-education chatbot prototype developed to make trustworthy public-health information easier to access. It uses retrieval techniques to find relevant material and makes the available sources visible, so people can explore the evidence behind a response.

✦

Clear explanations

Explore general questions about vaccines, the illnesses they help prevent, and why Vaccine Information Statements matter.

↗

Source transparency

Follow links to public CDC resources. For important health decisions, check the current guidance directly.

♡

Responsible boundaries

No patient records, diagnoses, prescriptions, or individualized vaccine recommendations. Clinical decisions belong with clinicians.

How it works

A simpler path from question to understanding.

The assistant sits in the corner of this site as a guided conversation widget — the rest of the page introduces the project, its goals and its limitations.

Important distinction: This public demo uses a small curated set of CDC-linked summaries with BM25 and a TF-IDF similarity proxy. Separately, the research pipeline indexed 163 CDC pages using MiniLM embeddings and Chroma. The demo is not that full research system.

Safety & responsible AI

Built to educate, not diagnose.

CareBridge is a research and educational prototype. It is not a medical device or a replacement for professional medical guidance.

  • ✓Only ask general vaccine-education questions; never enter private patient information.
  • ✓For personal vaccine eligibility, allergies, pregnancy or clinical decisions, consult a licensed healthcare professional.
  • ✓Verify the date and meaning of linked CDC guidance; answers can be incomplete or outdated.
  • ✓For a medical emergency in the United States, call 911.
A little more context

Frequently asked questions

Who is CareBridge for?

Students, learners and anyone who wants to explore general vaccine information. It is not a tool for making decisions about an individual patient's care.

Does it use the CDC as a source?

The public demo uses curated educational summaries linked to CDC pages. The separate academic research pipeline uses a larger saved and indexed CDC corpus. A URL does not guarantee that every claim is supported, so check the linked sources.

Can CareBridge tell me which vaccine I should receive?

No. Vaccination recommendations for a specific person depend on clinical history, age, timing, allergies and current guidance. Please consult a licensed healthcare professional.
